The Heart Bell did more than protect the animals. It remembered them.

Your role is no longer simply to free the Seventy-Seven: stabilize them, understand their habits, discover what is wrong, help them recover, form a bond, awaken their Pet God form, and finally release them into the Constellation Reserve. The deeper the hospital reactivates, the clearer the truth becomes: Aurelia was built as a living refuge, and the animals were its memory.
